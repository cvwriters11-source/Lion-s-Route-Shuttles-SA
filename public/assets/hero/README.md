# Hero image provenance

The seven fleet images below were generated specifically for the Lion's Route Shuttles SA website on 12 October 2026 using Cursor's image-generation tool, then prepared locally as 1600 × 900 optimized JPEGs.

They are fictional, representative concept imagery. The vehicles and professional adult drivers shown do not document or imply the company's exact fleet, employees, or inventory. No third-party source photography was used.

The generated source photography (1280 × 720) contained clean, unbranded white vehicles. No image model drew any logo or lettering.

## Fleet livery (revised 12 October 2026)

The first version pasted the full logo JPEG, white background included, onto each vehicle as a flat white rectangle. All seven images were rebuilt from the original clean, unbranded generated bases:

1. The supplied logo (`/public/assets/lions-route-logo.jpg`) was cut out to transparent artwork: the white card and dark frame were removed, the charcoal type was re-inked so it has no light fringe, and the gold lion keeps its original colours. The reusable files are in `/public/assets/brand/`.
2. Two vehicle decal lockups were assembled from that exact artwork, without redrawing it: the lion mark with the wordmark set on two lines (`LION'S ROUTE` / `SHUTTLES SA`). The tagline and descriptor lines were left off because they would be illegibly small on the vehicles.
3. Each clean base was upscaled to 1600 × 900. The decal was then perspective-warped onto a clear body panel, away from windows, handles, wheel arches and the driver, and blended into the paint (multiply against the panel's own shading, keeping fine specular highlights) so lighting, reflections, creases and grain run through it. There is no white box, shadow or glow.

## Hyundai Staria (added 13 October 2026)

`lions-route-staria.jpg` (Hyundai Staria at a Mpumalanga guest house) is the featured tile in the Services page hero. It was generated on 13 October 2026 with Cursor's image-generation tool as a clean, unbranded white Staria with a fictional professional adult driver (1280 × 720). The model was told not to draw any badge, logo or lettering. Two earlier drafts were rejected: one had a manufacturer badge on the nose, and the other had the driver blocking the sliding door.

It was then upscaled to 1600 × 900 and branded with the same process as the vehicles above. The horizontal lockup is perspective-warped onto the sliding door, below the handle and clear of the windows, door seams and wheel arch, and blended into the paint. There is no box, shadow or glow. The Home page carousel still uses `lions-route-luxury-minibus.jpg`.
